01

The goal

Both players start with 40 Life. Bring your opponent's Life to 0 and you win.

Most damage comes from creatures attacking your opponent directly. Some cards also deal damage or have their own special ways to win.

Running out of cards doesn't lose the game. If you need to draw from an empty deck, you take fatigue damage instead: 1 the first time, then 2, then 3, and so on.

02

Starting a duel

  • Both decks and both Fusion decks are shuffled.
  • Each player draws an opening hand of 7 cards.
  • A random player goes first.
  • There is no hand size limit, so you never have to discard for holding too many cards.

03

Your turn

At the start of your turn, all of your cards unseal. Then the turn moves through four phases:

  1. 1

    Draw

    Click your Deck to draw 1 card. You must draw before you can move on.

  2. 2

    Main

    Put 1 card into Mana, summon creatures, play Spells and Fields, and use Seal abilities.

  3. 3

    Battle

    Attack with your creatures. Your opponent can block.

  4. 4

    End

    You can still play cards and place Mana. Then end your turn.

When a turn ends, every creature on both sides heals back to full DEF. Damage on creatures never carries over between turns.

04

Mana

Mana is how you pay for cards. Any card from your hand can be put into your Mana zone, once per turn, during Main or End.

To play a card you need both of these:

◆

A matching attribute

At least one card in your Mana must share the card's attribute (Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Light or Dark). Sealed Mana still counts.

◇

Enough unsealed Mana

Pay the card's Mana cost by sealing that many unsealed Mana cards. Any attribute can pay the cost.

Example

You want to summon a Fire creature that costs 3. Your Mana holds 1 Fire card and 3 Water cards, all unsealed. You have Fire, so the attribute check passes. You seal any 3 of your Mana cards to pay. Next turn they all unseal again.

Mixing attributes in your Mana early on opens up more of your hand. Your first card of a new attribute is often the best one to put into Mana.

05

Sealed & unsealed

Every card in play is either unsealed (ready) or sealed (used up for this turn). Your cards unseal at the start of your turn.

Unsealed

  • Mana can pay costs
  • Creatures can attack or block
  • Cards can use a Seal ability
  • Creatures can't be attacked (usually)

Sealed

  • Mana can't pay costs (but still gives its attribute)
  • Creatures can't attack or block
  • A creature becomes sealed after it attacks or blocks
  • Creatures can be attacked

This is the key to combat: a creature you used this turn stays sealed during your opponent's turn, so it's open to attack.

07

Card types

Creature

Summon it from your hand into an empty Battle slot. Creatures have ATK (damage dealt) and DEF (health). You can summon as many as your Mana and slots allow.

Browse creatures →

Spell

Placed in a Spell slot. A normal Spell resolves and goes to the Graveyard. Field Spells stay in play, and Equipment attaches to a creature. Some Spells, like Counter Spell, are only used as responses.

Browse spells →

Fusion

Powerful creatures kept in your Fusion deck. You bring them out by combining material cards, usually with a Fusion Spell.

Browse fusions →

Summoning sickness: a creature can't attack or use most abilities on the turn it's summoned, unless it has Haste.

08

Combat

In your Battle phase, each unsealed creature that's been out since your last turn can attack once.

⚔

Attack the player

If it isn't blocked, your opponent loses Life equal to your creature's ATK.

✦

Attack a creature

You can only target a sealed enemy creature (unless a card says otherwise).

  • Blocking: your opponent can block any attack, including direct ones, with one of their unsealed creatures.
  • Damage: the two creatures hit each other at the same time. Each deals its ATK to the other's DEF.
  • A creature whose DEF drops to 0 is destroyed and goes to the Graveyard.
  • Creature fights never damage Life. Only unblocked attacks on the player do.
  • Attackers and blockers become sealed.

Example

Your ATK 5 / DEF 3 creature attacks. Your opponent blocks with an ATK 2 / DEF 4 creature. Yours deals 5 to its 4 DEF, so the blocker is destroyed. It deals 2 back to your 3 DEF, so your creature survives and heals at the end of the turn.

09

Keywords

Card effects start with a keyword that says when they happen, like Battlecry; Draw 1 card. Tap a keyword to see cards that use it.

Battlecry
Happens when the creature is summoned.
Play
Happens when the card is played.
Seal
An ability you choose to use on your turn (Main or End). Using it seals the card, so it's once per turn. You can't use it the turn the card comes into play.
Death
Happens when the creature is destroyed.
Quick
Like Seal, but you can also use it on your opponent's turn as a response.
Hand
Used straight from your hand as a response, for example to stop an effect or an attack.
Haste
Ignores summoning sickness, so the creature can attack the turn it arrives.
Discarded
Happens when the card is discarded from your hand.
Roll
Roll a six-sided die. The effect depends on the result.
Fusion
On a Fusion card, lists the materials needed to summon it.

Some cards also use tokens, small counters placed on cards. For example, Exp tokens can level a card up, and a Shield token is used up instead of the creature being destroyed.

10

Responses & chains

When a Spell or effect is played, your opponent gets a chance to respond first, with a Hand card, a Quick effect, or a card like Counter Spell.

  1. 1You play a Spell
  2. 2Opponent responds with Counter Spell
  3. 3You respond again…

Players keep taking turns to respond until both pass. Then the chain resolves from the last card added back to the first, so the newest response happens first.

11

Fusion

Fusion creatures wait in your Fusion deck until you combine the right materials.

  • Play a Fusion Spell and choose a Fusion card whose recipe you can meet.
  • Pick the material cards it lists. They can come from your hand or the field.
  • The materials and the Fusion Spell go to the Graveyard, and the Fusion creature is summoned.
  • If your Battle zone is full, at least one material must come from it to free a slot.

Open a Fusion card in the archive and check its Related Cards to see its materials.

12

Building a deck

60–80cards in your main deck
0–10cards in your Fusion deck
3copies of a card, at most
100deck points, at most

Each card has a points value (shown as PTS in the archive). Stronger cards cost more points. Every copy in both your main and Fusion decks counts toward the 100-point budget, so you have to choose your power cards carefully.

Try it: the Deck workshop in the archive tracks your counts and points as you add cards.

13

First-game tips

  • Play the in-game Training. The TRAINING button on the main menu walks you through real moves in three short chapters: Basics, Effects and Advanced.
  • Place Mana every turn. Missing a Mana drop slows you down for the whole game.
  • Watch what's sealed. Attacking with everything leaves your creatures sealed and open to attack on your opponent's turn.
  • Keep a blocker unsealed when you're low on Life.
  • Read Battlecry and Death effects. A creature that looks weak may do a lot when it arrives or when it's destroyed.
